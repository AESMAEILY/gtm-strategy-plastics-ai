# gtm-strategy-plastics-ai
GTM Strategy: Plastics.ai Academic Expansion in Germany
Course project for the Customer Centricity module at SDI Munich (2026). A go-to-market strategy for driving university adoption of Plastics.ai across German higher education institutions.
Scope
Market segmentation of German universities and applied sciences institutions, persona development across 5 academic user types, value proposition design, competitive use-case analysis (Plastics.ai vs ChatGPT), customer journey mapping, and a three-tier pricing offer structure.
Key Outputs
Market funnel narrowing from 52,000 professors nationally to 130 in plastics/polymer science. Five personas covering professors, junior research group leaders, IT administrators, and bachelor/master students. Three institutional offer models: Campus Lab License, Academic Access Pack, and a bundled eLearning package with Hanser, ranging from €1,500 to €28,000+ annually.
Tools & Methods
Customer journey mapping, persona modeling, Jobs-to-be-Done framework, Target Cross analysis, keyword strategy, value proposition canvas.
Team: Anna Ellinger, Philipp Simonides, Patrick Hummel, Danish Pervez, Alireza Esmaeily
